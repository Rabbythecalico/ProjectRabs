ProjectRabs

Welcome!

ProjectRabs is a sanctuary for original character creators. Whether you're a writer, artist, roleplayer, or worldbuilder, this platform gives you the space to bring your OCs to life—your way.

---

🌈 LGBTQ+ Friendly

We believe every identity deserves to be seen and respected. Our platform is built with inclusivity at its core:

· Custom pronouns and gender fields
· Non-binary and fluid identity support
· Relationship fields with no gender restrictions
· Safe and welcoming community guidelines

---

🎨 Fully Customizable

Your characters are unique—your pages should be too. We give you total creative control:

· Drag-and-drop builder – rearrange sections effortlessly
· Custom CSS/HTML – for complete design freedom
· Color schemes – light, dark, and custom accents
· Modular layouts – mix and match bio, gallery, stats, lore, and more

---

✨ Features at a Glance

· Character profiles with unlimited fields
· Image galleries and art uploads
· World and story organization
· Tagging and filtering system
· Content warnings for sensitive topics
· Private and public visibility options

🚀 Getting Started

1. Sign up for a free account
2. Create your first character profile
3. Customize your layout and design
4. Share with the community or keep it private
